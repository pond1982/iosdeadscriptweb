Dead Script for Mac privacy policy

This privacy policy covers the native macOS edition of Dead Script, published by Suwitcha Sugthana.

Playing on your Mac

The game works offline without an account. The developer does not collect or transmit your gameplay data through the app. The app does not use advertising, analytics or tracking services. The narration, music and sound effects are included in the download.

Local records

The game saves chapter bookmarks, lesson progress, best lesson accuracy, settings and encounter results in its local app data. In the optional first-time playtest it saves a report on your Mac. The report contains stage timings, mistake counts, milestones, and encounter outcomes. It does not contain a transcript of what you typed. Reports stay in your local app data until you remove them. The game keeps no developer-side copy of these records.

At the results screen, Show in Finder reveals the report. You can delete that file in Finder. Copy report puts the text on your clipboard. You decide whether to share it.

Contacting support

If you email the developer, the message includes your email address and any details or attachments you choose to send. The developer uses that information to answer your request. To ask about privacy or request deletion of a support message, write to pond1982a@gmail.com.
