The Morgue of Deleted Scenes

On the archive steps, a dying earlier draft of Elias opens his hand. The Golden Ribbon inside fits the revolver. Down in the Filing Aisle, each correct letter fires a shot. At the Red Fissure, each finished word gives Elias another plank to stand on.

The Rotting Rotogravure

The cyan presses stamp copies of a Deadline in rows. On the drying line, a folded Errata springs between words. When the Heavy-Handed Critic crosses out a line, type STET to insist that it stands.

The Wharf of Wasted Ink

Magenta ink rises between the piers, taking the lettering with it. Work the signal lantern, track the surviving copies and learn when to cite the original.

The Bindery of Bound Souls

In the Bindery, a word can be sewn to another word. Follow the thread, unpick the binding and cut the chain before the Bookbinder closes her ledger.

The Blank Page

Beyond the dead-letter lift, names lose their ink. Hold each fading word long enough to anchor it to the page. At the top of the Tower, Elias must face the Author of Oakhaven.

Player guide

At the keys

Everything you need to play is on your Mac keyboard.

TypingCombatSettings

Start at Elias’s desk

Choose Learn to type for 33 lessons on an English QWERTY keyboard. Follow the lit finger and copy the faint letters onto the paper.

In the first-time playtest, two F/J lines feed straight into the clerk’s proof. Press Delete to lift the stray letter, then copy the note.

Fire with a word

Choose Begin story to follow Elias. Type the word on the Golden Ribbon handoff, then the word above each enemy. The first threat waits for your first correct letter.

A mistype preserves the letters you have landed. Press Esc to pause and Return to resume.

Set your pace

Press ⌘ + , for Settings. Relaxed pace doubles the enemy and parry windows. Large Type increases the size of words and captions.

Reduce Motion and Reduce Flash work independently. Music, effects and Elias’s narration each have their own volume control.

How do I continue a saved game?

Choose Continue on the title screen. After quitting, the campaign resumes at its saved passage or chapter. Office lessons keep a separate bookmark.

Where is my playtest report?

Choose Show in Finder on the results screen to reveal the Markdown file, or Copy report to copy its contents. Early exits save a partial report. You choose whether to share it.

Can I change the sound, flashes or typing pace?

Yes. Open Settings with ⌘ + ,. You can adjust narration, music and effects separately, reduce flashes and motion, enlarge the type or choose Relaxed pace.

Does the game need an internet connection?

The game plays offline. The artwork, narration and music ship with the app. Progress and playtest records stay on your Mac. Read the Mac privacy policy.
