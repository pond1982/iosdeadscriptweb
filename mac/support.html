Dead Script for Mac support

For help with Dead Script on macOS, email pond1982a@gmail.com. Include your macOS version and what you saw on screen.

Start at the desk or the archive

Choose Learn to type to practice at Elias’s desk or Begin story for the full adventure. The desk uses an English QWERTY keyboard. Use the arrow keys or Tab to choose a menu item and press Return to open it.

Correct the clerk’s proof

In First-time playtest, complete the two F/J/Space lines. The clerk’s proof rolls in automatically. Press the Mac Delete key to lift the crossed-out lowercase e, then type the faint sentence, including its capital and punctuation.

Change pace, text and sound

Press Command-comma to open Settings. Relaxed pace gives you more time during fights. You can enlarge target words, reduce motion or flashes, and adjust narration, music and effects separately.

Find a saved game or playtest report

Choose Continue on the title screen to resume at your campaign bookmark. Lessons keep a separate record. At the end of a first-time playtest, choose Show in Finder to reveal its Markdown report or Copy report to copy the text. Include a report in a support message only if you want to share it.
